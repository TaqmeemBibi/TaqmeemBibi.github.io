Welcome to My Research Profile

I am a PhD scholar at Beijing Forestry University, Beijing, China, specializing in applied mathematics and computational fluid dynamics. My research focuses on numerical analysis of partial differential equations, hybrid nanofluids, entropy generation, and heat/mass transfer processes. I am passionate about developing efficient computational methods for sustainable energy systems.
