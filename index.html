Welcome to My Academic Hub

I am a PhD scholar at Beijing Forestry University, specializing in applied mathematics and fluid dynamics. My research focuses on numerical analysis of partial differential equations (PDEs), hybrid nanofluids, entropy generation, and heat/mass transfer processes. Through advanced mathematical modeling and computational techniques, I aim to contribute to sustainable energy systems.
