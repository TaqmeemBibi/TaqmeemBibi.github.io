Research Overview

My research focuses on the intersection of applied mathematics and fluid mechanics, with an emphasis on numerical analysis of partial differential equations (PDEs) governing complex fluid flow phenomena. I specialize in the study of hybrid nanofluids, entropy generation, and heat/mass transfer processes, aiming to develop efficient computational methods for sustainable energy systems and advanced thermal management solutions.

Active Research Projects Active

Hybrid Nanofluid Flow with Micro-organisms
Numerical analysis of hybrid nanofluid containing motile microorganisms under Cattaneo-Christov double diffusion model. Investigating heat and mass transfer enhancement in porous media.
Active
Entropy Generation in MHD Flows
Analysis of entropy production in magnetohydrodynamic flows with hybrid nanoparticles. Focus on optimization of thermal systems and irreversibility minimization.
Active
Numerical Methods for Nonlinear PDEs
Development of advanced finite difference and finite element methods for solving nonlinear partial differential equations arising in fluid dynamics.
